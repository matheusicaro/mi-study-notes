#### TITLE: HBC IN-STORE EMAIL VERIFICATION AND FLOW UPDATES JUN > SEP 2023

##### DESCRIPTION

I worked on the project: HBC In-Store Email Verification and Flow Updates,
The HBC in-store flow has historically driven one-and-done customers while also being our highest fraud channel. The in-store handoff flow we created is the first step to solving both of those issues, where we get folks to claim their profile to allow us to run innovation and get them more engaged with the product. However, the adoption of this flow has been too low (~7%) to see massive improvements in either problem channel. With that, we started redesigning the flow with new user validations, email and phone.

##### MY ROLE

My role was intermediate full-stack developer, no changes.
<Br/>
The project started in July with 2 Devs and 1 product:

- Connor Newman - Product
- Max Gooding - Tech leader
- John Lendvoy - responsive for Front-end tasks
  But after the first 2/3 weeks (something before August), Max Gooding had to leave for a license of 3 months. With that, I was the only dev responsible for the architecture of the back-end. During this time, I was able to deliver all the tickets, fixes, and improvements during the project.

##### IMPACT

We can reduce 5 digits with spending on fraud in the first months.
We also have a >30% improvement in catching fraudsters in the new flow
We’ve raised the number of users with fully verified accounts by ~10x :air_horn:
We’ve cut the transactions hitting fraud review by ~50% :air_horn:
